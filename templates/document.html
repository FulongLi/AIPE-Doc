ENGINEERING NOTE
01 / CONTEXT & APPROACH

Make complex ideas clear.

A useful technical note answers three questions: why this work matters, how it was done, and what the results mean. Establish the context and scope, then explain the method, evidence and limitations.

This is body text. Add key points, technical terms, units and equations such as P = V × I. Link to original sources in the references. Use local SVG images for complex equations that must remain sharp offline and in print.

Problem & objectives

Define the problem, intended readers and use case. State the inputs, outputs and acceptance criteria.

Method & boundaries

Record the approach and assumptions. Distinguish illustrative data, calculations and measurements so others can check the work.

Table 1. Content structure example (not measured engineering data)

Key takeaway

Every significant claim should be traceable to evidence. Cite figure sources, include units, and clearly label anything that has not yet been validated.
